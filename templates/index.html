What it does

01

Abstract classifier

Labels each sentence as background, objective, methods, results or conclusions, with score distributions and the terms behind each call.

02

Entity extraction

Highlights chemicals and diseases with BioBERT, with per-entity confidence and category statistics.

03

Summarization studio

Chunks long papers, summarises locally and puts the original beside the result.

04

Research explorer

All three at once in a single exportable report (PDF or TXT).
